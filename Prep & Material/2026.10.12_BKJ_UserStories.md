## Story 1 — Create Sales Order with Milestone Billing Plan

**Priority:** High
**Story Points:** 3

As an Internal Sales Representative,
I want to create a sales order that includes a milestone billing plan with a down payment line and a final invoice line,
so that the agreed payment schedule is captured in the system at the point of order entry and drives all subsequent billing steps automatically.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Sales order saves correctly with item category CBAO (or CTAD / CTAX for service and non-stock materials)
- Billing plan contains exactly two date lines: down payment (date category 04) and final invoice (date category 02)
- The system calculates the final invoice amount automatically; amounts across both lines sum to the total order value
- Both billing plan lines carry a billing block immediately after order creation

---

## Story 2 — Release Billing Block for Down Payment

**Priority:** High
**Story Points:** 2

As an Internal Sales Representative,
I want to manually remove the billing block from the down payment date line of the billing plan,
so that the Billing Clerk can process the down payment request while the final invoice line remains protected.

Acceptance Criteria:
- After the release action, the down payment date line shows no billing block
- The final invoice date line retains its billing block unchanged
- If both blocks are absent after this step, the system state is incorrect and must be flagged as a defect

---

## Story 3 — Create Down Payment Request (FAZ)

**Priority:** High
**Story Points:** 2

As a Billing Clerk,
I want to create a down payment request billing document (type FAZ) from the billing due list,
so that the customer receives a formal request for the agreed advance payment tied to the sales order.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The FAZ billing document is created and references the originating sales order in the document flow
- The billing document amount matches the down payment amount defined in the billing plan
- The financial posting goes to the down payment receivable account, not to a revenue account
- Output condition is present on the billing document and a print preview is available

---

## Story 4 — Post and Clear Incoming Down Payment

**Priority:** High
**Story Points:** 3

As an Accounts Receivable Accountant,
I want to post the customer's incoming payment and clear it against the open down payment request via the special G/L transaction path,
so that the system records receipt of the advance payment and enables the delivery to proceed.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The incoming payment is posted successfully using the Post Incoming Payments app
- The open FAZ item is found via the special G/L transactions filter and cleared
- The journal entry debits the bank account and credits the down payment liability account
- After clearing, the delivery gate condition is met (delivery block removed automatically or manually as per configuration)

---

## Story 5 — Enforce Delivery Block Until Payment Is Cleared

**Priority:** Very High
**Story Points:** 2

As a Shipping Specialist,
I want the system to prevent me from creating an outbound delivery before the down payment is confirmed as received,
so that goods are never dispatched without the agreed financial precondition being met.

Acceptance Criteria:
- An attempt to create a delivery before down payment clearance results in a system block with a clear, informative message
- Delivery creation completes successfully after the down payment has been posted and cleared
- No workaround (e.g. manual removal of schedule line block) is possible without correct authorisation configuration
- The control applies regardless of whether the delivery block is removed automatically or manually

## Story 6 — Create Delivery and Execute Picking

**Priority:** Medium
**Story Points:** 2

As a Shipping Specialist,
I want to create an outbound delivery from the confirmed sales order and record the picked quantity,
so that the physical goods are staged for shipment and the delivery is ready for goods issue.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The delivery appears in the correct worklist view ("For Picking") for the Shipping Specialist role
- Delivery creation succeeds when sufficient stock is available; system blocks with a clear message if stock is insufficient
- Picked quantity matches the delivery quantity before the delivery can be saved
- For batch-managed materials, a batch number is assigned before saving

---

## Story 7 — Post Goods Issue

**Priority:** Medium
**Story Points:** 1

As a Shipping Specialist,
I want to post goods issue against the outbound delivery,
so that the system records the physical departure of goods, reduces inventory, and enables revenue recognition.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Goods issue posts successfully with a confirmation message and posting date
- Inventory for the shipped material is reduced by the delivery quantity immediately after posting
- Financial posting debits cost of goods sold and credits the inventory account
- The goods issue material document appears in the document flow of the delivery

---

## Story 8 — Release Final Invoice Block and Create Final Invoice (F2)

**Priority:** Very High
**Story Points:** 3

As a Billing Clerk,
I want to create the final invoice after the Internal Sales Representative has released the billing block post goods issue,
so that the customer is billed for the remaining balance with the advance payment correctly deducted.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The final invoice billing block cannot be removed before goods issue is posted; the attempt results in a block or the line does not appear in the billing due list
- After the Internal Sales Representative removes the final invoice billing block, the line appears in the billing due list for the Billing Clerk
- The final invoice (type F2) shows the down payment as a distinct deduction line item
- The net amount due on the final invoice equals the order value minus all cleared down payments, with zero deviation
- The financial posting clears the down payment liability account and posts the remaining receivable

---

## Story 9 — End-to-End Document Flow Traceability

**Priority:** Medium
**Story Points:** 2

As any process participant (Internal Sales Representative, Billing Clerk, Accounts Receivable Accountant, or Shipping Specialist),
I want to navigate the complete document chain from any point in the process,
so that I can trace the full transaction history for operational queries, audits, or error resolution.

Acceptance Criteria:
- From the final invoice, the full document flow is navigable back to the originating sales order without breaks: final invoice → outbound delivery → goods issue document → down payment journal entry → down payment request (FAZ) → sales order
- Each document in the chain displays the correct reference to the preceding and succeeding documents
- No document in the chain is orphaned (i.e. exists without a valid reference in at least one direction)
- Document flow navigation is available to all four process roles within their respective apps
