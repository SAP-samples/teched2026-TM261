# Solution Design Document: Sales Order Processing with Customer Down Payment (BKJ)

_Process: S/4HANA Sales Order Processing with Customer Down Payment (Scope Item BKJ)_
_Based on: BKJ_RequirementsDocument.md · BKJ_UserStories.md · BKJ Test Script (ToKe001)_

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This document specifies the solution design for implementing the standard SAP S/4HANA process **Sales Order Processing with Customer Down Payment** (Scope Item BKJ) within the project scope.

The process extends standard order-to-cash by introducing a structured billing plan (milestone billing) that enforces collection of an agreed advance payment before goods are dispatched. The Billing Clerk issues a down payment request billing document; the Accounts Receivable Accountant posts the incoming payment and clears the open item; only after that clearance does the system allow delivery creation. A final invoice is subsequently issued with the advance payment deducted as a distinct line item.

The solution is delivered entirely through **standard SAP configuration and standard Fiori applications**. No custom development is required.

---

## 2. Business Context

### 2.1 Problem Statement

Standard sales order processing does not enforce upfront payment collection before goods shipment. For high-value transactions or orders with new customers, this exposes the business to:

- Unprotected cash risk on large orders shipped before payment is secured
- Manual workarounds (spreadsheet tracking, informal approvals) with no audit trail
- Inconsistent application of down payment terms across the sales organization

### 2.2 Business Objectives

| # | Objective | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Enforce advance payment collection before delivery | Delivery creation blocked 100% of the time until down payment is cleared |
| O2 | Ensure correct financial representation of down payments | Final invoice shows down payment deduction with zero deviation |
| O3 | Maintain full document traceability | End-to-end document flow navigable from any document in the chain |
| O4 | Minimize manual effort through system-driven controls | Billing block management and delivery gating handled by standard config |

### 2.3 Stakeholders

| Role | Responsibility in Process |
|---|---|
| Internal Sales Representative | Creates sales order with billing plan; releases billing blocks |
| Billing Clerk | Creates down payment request (FAZ) and final invoice (F2) |
| Accounts Receivable Accountant | Posts and clears incoming down payment |
| Shipping Specialist | Creates delivery, executes picking, posts goods issue |
| Warehouse Clerk | Sets initial stock (prerequisite only; not part of main flow) |
| IT / Basis | System configuration, role assignments, transport management |

## 3. Process Design

### 3.1 Process Flow (To-Be)

```
[Internal Sales Rep]
        |
        v
Create Sales Order (VA01)
with Milestone Billing Plan
        |
        v
Remove Billing Block
(Down Payment line only)
        |
        v
[Billing Clerk]
        |
        v
Create Down Payment Request (FAZ)
        |
        v
[AR Accountant]
        |
        v
Post Incoming Down Payment
(Clear open FAZ item)
        |
        v
[Shipping Specialist]
        |
        v
Create Outbound Delivery ──── (BLOCKED if down payment not cleared)
        |
        v
Execute Picking
        |
        v
Post Goods Issue
        |
        v
[Internal Sales Rep]
        |
        v
Remove Billing Block
(Final Invoice line)
        |
        v
[Billing Clerk]
        |
        v
Create Final Invoice (F2)
with Down Payment Deduction
        |
        v
End Process
```

Succeeding processes (separate scope): Accounts Receivable / Incoming Payment (J59) · Sales Order Fulfillment Monitoring (BKK)

### 3.2 Roles and Handover Points

| Handover | From | To | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Sales Rep | Billing Clerk | Billing block removed on down payment line |
| 2 | Billing Clerk | AR Accountant | FAZ billing document created and sent to customer |
| 3 | AR Accountant | Shipping Specialist | Down payment cleared; delivery gate open |
| 4 | Shipping Specialist | Internal Sales Rep | Goods issue posted |
| 5 | Internal Sales Rep | Billing Clerk | Billing block removed on final invoice line |

### 3.3 Integration Points

| System / Component | Direction | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| SD (Sales & Distribution) | Core | Sales order, billing plan, billing documents |
| FI (Financial Accounting) | Outbound from SD | Financial postings at down payment request, payment, goods issue, final invoice |
| MM / WM (Inventory) | Outbound from SD | Inventory reduction at goods issue |
| Output Management | Outbound | PDF output for down payment request and final invoice |
| Document Flow (SD) | Internal | End-to-end traceability across all documents |

## 5. Functional Specification

### 5.1 Create Sales Order with Milestone Billing Plan

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Manage Sales Orders (F1873) / VA01 |
| **Role** | Internal Sales Representative |
| **Trigger** | Customer order received with agreed down payment terms |
| **Precondition** | Stock available (or planned); customer master exists; billing plan type configured |
| **Key Inputs** | Sales document type (e.g. OR), Sales Org 1010, Distribution Channel 10, Division 00, Sold-to Party, Material, Quantity, Item Category |

**Item Category Selection:**

| Material Type | Item Category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Trading Good (HAWA) | CBAO | Milestone Billing Plan |
| Service Material | CTAD | Milestone Billing Plan – Service |
| Non-Stock Material | CTAX | Milestone Billing Plan – Non-Stock |

**Billing Plan Configuration:**

| Line | Date Category | Content | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Down Payment | 04 | Billing date + amount/percentage | User |
| Final Invoice | 02 | Billing date; amount calculated automatically | System |

**Postcondition:** Sales order saved; billing plan contains two date lines summing to order value; billing block present on both lines.

---

### 5.2 Release Billing Block – Down Payment

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Manage Sales Orders (F1873) / VA02 |
| **Role** | Internal Sales Representative |
| **Trigger** | Down payment terms confirmed; ready for billing |
| **Navigation** | Sales Order → Item → Billing Plan → Dates section → Block column |
| **Action** | Delete billing block on Down Payment date line only |

**Postcondition:** Down payment line: no billing block. Final invoice line: billing block retained.

---

### 5.3 Create Down Payment Request (FAZ)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Create Billing Documents (F0798) |
| **Role** | Billing Clerk |
| **Trigger** | Down payment line appears in billing due list |
| **Billing Settings** | Set billing date and type before billing: ON; Separate billing document per item: ON; Auto-post: ON |
| **Billing Type** | FAZ (Down Payment Request) |

**Postcondition:** FAZ billing document created and posted; references originating sales order in document flow; financial posting to down payment receivable account (e.g. 21191000 in demo systems; verify against chart of accounts in productive systems); output condition present for PDF generation.

---

### 5.4 Post and Clear Incoming Down Payment

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Post Incoming Payments (F1345) |
| **Role** | Accounts Receivable Accountant |
| **Trigger** | Customer payment received |
| **Key Inputs** | Company Code, Posting Date, Journal Entry Type DZ, G/L Account (Bank), House Bank, Amount, Customer Account |
| **Open Item Filter** | Special G/L Transactions (required to find FAZ items) |
| **Action** | Select FAZ open item; choose Clear; Post |

**Postcondition:** Incoming payment journal entry posted; FAZ open item cleared; delivery gate condition met (delivery block removed automatically if configured, or manually).

---

### 5.5 Delivery Gate Enforcement

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Enforced by** | SD delivery block on sales order schedule lines |
| **Mechanism** | Delivery block set automatically at billing plan date category 04 (configurable); removed on down payment clearance (automatic if configured via billing plan type, otherwise manual) |
| **Expected system behavior** | Delivery creation attempt before clearance: system blocks with informative message. After clearance: delivery creation proceeds normally. |

**Acceptance check:** Attempt delivery creation before payment clearance and confirm block; repeat after clearance and confirm success.

---

### 5.6 Create Outbound Delivery and Execute Picking

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Create Outbound Deliveries From Sales Orders (F0869A) |
| **Role** | Shipping Specialist |
| **Key Inputs** | Shipping Point (e.g. 1010), Planned Creation Date, Sales Order Number |
| **Precondition** | Down payment cleared; sufficient stock available |

**Picking:**

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | My Outbound Delivery Monitor (VL06O) → For Picking |
| **Action** | Enter picked quantity per item (and batch if applicable); Save |
| **Postcondition** | Picked quantity = delivery quantity; delivery status updated to picking complete |

---

### 5.7 Post Goods Issue

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | My Outbound Delivery Monitor (VL06O) → For Goods Issue |
| **Role** | Shipping Specialist |
| **Key Inputs** | Shipping Point 1010; select delivery; Post GI; confirm posting date |
| **Postcondition** | Delivery status: goods issued; inventory reduced by delivery quantity; COGS posting generated; goods issue material document linked in document flow |

---

### 5.8 Release Billing Block – Final Invoice

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Manage Sales Orders (F1873) / VA02 |
| **Role** | Internal Sales Representative |
| **Trigger** | Goods issue confirmed; COGS recognized |
| **Precondition** | Goods issue must be posted; billing block on final invoice line must still be present |
| **Action** | Navigate to billing plan → Final Invoice date line → Delete billing block; Save |

**Postcondition:** Final invoice line: no billing block; line appears in billing due list for Billing Clerk.

---

### 5.9 Create Final Invoice (F2)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **App** | Create Billing Documents (F0798) |
| **Role** | Billing Clerk |
| **Billing Type** | F2 (Invoice) |
| **Key Output** | Final invoice showing full order amount with down payment as a separate deduction line item |

**Postcondition:** F2 billing document created; net amount due = order value minus cleared down payments (zero deviation); down payment liability account cleared; remaining receivable posted; document flow complete end to end.

## 9. Authorization Concept

No custom authorization objects are required. The process uses standard SAP business roles.

| Business Role | Role ID | Process Access |
|---|---|---|
| Internal Sales Representative | SAP_BR_INTERNAL_SALES_REP | Create/change sales orders; release billing blocks |
| Billing Clerk | SAP_BR_BILLING_CLERK | Create billing documents (FAZ, F2); manage billing due list |
| Accounts Receivable Accountant | SAP_BR_AR_ACCOUNTANT | Post incoming payments; clear down payment open items |
| Shipping Specialist | SAP_BR_SHIPPING_SPECIALIST | Create deliveries; execute picking; post goods issue |
| Warehouse Clerk | SAP_BR_WAREHOUSE_CLERK | Post goods movement (initial stock setup) |
| Configuration Expert – BPC | SAP_BR_BPC_EXPERT | Access to customizing (setup only) |

Roles must be assigned to individual test users prior to process execution. Launchpad spaces per role are defined in the standard SAP Best Practices role documentation.

| Availability | Same as SD/FI module | Per system SLA |

## 11. Error Handling

| Error Condition | System Behavior | Expected User Message / Action |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery creation attempted before down payment cleared | System block; delivery not created | Message indicating delivery block; user must confirm payment clearance first |
| Wrong billing type selected (F2 instead of FAZ for down payment) | Billing plan logic breaks; down payment not recognized as prepayment | Test should verify FAZ is the only valid type at this stage |
| Down payment amount exceeds order value | Billing plan validation should prevent entry | System validation message; entry rejected |
| Billing block not removed before billing run | Item does not appear in billing due list | Clerk sees no result; must notify sales rep to release block |
| Goods issue posted but final invoice billing block not removed | Final invoice line absent from billing due list | Billing Clerk cannot proceed; must request block removal |
| Stock insufficient at delivery creation | Delivery creation fails | System message indicating insufficient stock; prerequisite stock posting required |
| Cancellation of FAZ after incoming payment posted | Cancellation blocked | System requires payment reversal before billing document cancellation |
| Multiple down payments on one order | Final invoice must deduct all cleared down payments | Verify F2 net amount = order value minus sum of all down payments |

## 14. Out of Scope

- Changes to pricing conditions or the condition technique
- Changes to credit management or credit limit checks
- Integration with external payment processing systems
- Accounts receivable dunning process
- Background scheduling of delivery or billing creation
- Sales Order Fulfillment Monitoring (BKK) — separate scope item
- Accounts Receivable incoming final payment (J59) — separate scope item
- Batch management configuration (used only if batch-managed materials are selected)
- Custom development of any kind

## 15. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Billing Plan | A schedule of billing milestones attached to a sales order item, defining when and how much to bill |
| Billing Block | A flag on a billing plan date line that prevents the line from appearing in the billing due list |
| Date Category | A configuration key on a billing plan line that controls billing behavior (04 = down payment, 02 = final invoice) |
| Document Flow | The linked chain of SD and FI documents created during a business transaction, navigable in both directions |
| FAZ | SAP billing document type for a down payment request |
| F2 | SAP billing document type for a standard customer invoice |
| Goods Issue (GI) | The system event recording the physical departure of goods from the warehouse; triggers inventory reduction and COGS posting |
| Item Category | A configuration key on a sales order item that determines billing and delivery behavior (CBAO, CTAD, CTAX) |
| Milestone Billing | A billing plan variant where billing events are tied to project or process milestones rather than delivery |
| Open Item | An uncleared financial posting awaiting a clearing counterpart (e.g. a FAZ billing document awaiting incoming payment) |
| Special G/L | A sub-ledger transaction type in FI used for items such as down payments, which post to a separate account from normal receivables |
| Scope Item BKJ | SAP Best Practices identifier for Sales Order Processing with Customer Down Payment |
