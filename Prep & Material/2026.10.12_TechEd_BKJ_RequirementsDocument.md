# Requirements Document: Sales Order Processing with Customer Down Payment (BKJ)

---

## 1. Business Background

The standard SAP S/4HANA process "Sales Order Processing with Customer Down Payment" (Scope Item BKJ) extends the standard order-to-cash flow to cover scenarios where a customer is required to provide partial payment before goods are shipped. This is typical for high-value transactions or when working with new customers where credit exposure needs to be limited.

The process uses SAP Billing Plan functionality (milestone billing) within Sales and Distribution. Rather than a single invoice at delivery, the billing sequence is split: a down payment request is issued first, payment is received and posted by Accounts Receivable, and only then is delivery authorized. A final invoice is issued after goods issue, with the advance payment deducted.

Without this capability, there is no automated mechanism to enforce upfront payment collection before shipment. The business is exposed to manual workarounds, delayed delivery authorization, or unprotected cash risk on large orders.

---

## 2. Business Requirement

As an Internal Sales Representative,
I want to create a sales order that includes a structured billing plan with a mandatory down payment,
so that the system automatically enforces collection of the agreed advance payment before any goods are dispatched.

---

## 3. Scope of Change

### 3.1 Standard Process (As-Is)

Create Sales Order → Create Delivery → Execute Picking → Post Goods Issue → Create Invoice

### 3.2 Enhanced Process (To-Be)

Create Sales Order with Billing Plan → Remove Billing Block (Down Payment) → Create Down Payment Request (FAZ) → Post Down Payment → Create Delivery → Execute Picking → Post Goods Issue → Remove Billing Block (Final Invoice) → Create Final Invoice (F2, with down payment deduction)

### 3.3 Out of Scope

- Changes to pricing conditions or the condition technique
- Changes to credit management or credit limit checks
- Integration with external payment processing systems
- Modifications to the accounts receivable dunning process
- Background scheduling of delivery or billing creation (optional config, not in scope)

## 4. Functional Requirements

Requirement: The system shall allow creation of a sales order with a milestone billing plan, supporting item categories CBAO (trading goods), CTAD (service materials), and CTAX (non-stock materials).
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The billing plan shall support at least two date lines — a down payment line (date category 04) and a final invoice line (date category 02) — with the system automatically calculating the final invoice amount based on the down payment value entered.
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The system shall apply a billing block to all billing plan date lines at order creation. The Internal Sales Representative shall manually release the down payment line before billing can proceed; the final invoice line shall remain blocked until after goods issue.
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The system shall prevent creation of an outbound delivery if the down payment has not been posted and cleared against the open item in Accounts Receivable.
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The Billing Clerk shall be able to create a down payment request billing document (type FAZ) from the billing due list, with the document referencing the originating sales order and posting to the down payment receivable account — not to a revenue account.
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The Accounts Receivable Accountant shall be able to post an incoming customer payment and clear it against the open down payment request via the special G/L transaction path in the Post Incoming Payments app.
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The final invoice (type F2) shall display the previously received down payment as a distinct deduction line item. The net amount due shall equal the order value minus all cleared down payments, with zero deviation from the system-calculated balance.
Priority: Must
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The system shall optionally support automatic removal of the delivery block upon down payment clearance, configurable per billing plan type.
Priority: Should
────────────────────────────────────────
Requirement: The system shall maintain a complete and navigable document flow linking all documents in the process: sales order, down payment request, payment journal entry, outbound delivery, goods issue document, and final invoice.
Priority: Should

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

1. A sales order with a milestone billing plan saves correctly with two date lines; amounts across both lines sum to the total order value, and the final invoice amount is calculated by the system without manual entry.
2. At order creation, both billing plan lines carry a billing block. After the sales rep releases the down payment line, only that line is unblocked; the final invoice line remains blocked.
3. An attempt to create an outbound delivery before the down payment is posted results in a system block with a clear message. Delivery creation completes successfully after the down payment is cleared in Accounts Receivable.
4. The down payment request (FAZ) references the originating sales order in the document flow and posts to the down payment receivable account, not to revenue. No revenue posting occurs at this stage.
5. The final invoice (F2) shows the down payment as a separate deduction line. The net amount payable equals order value minus all posted down payments, with zero deviation.
6. The complete document flow is navigable end to end: sales order → down payment request → payment journal entry → outbound delivery → goods issue document → final invoice.
7. A cancelled down payment request cannot be processed while an open incoming payment exists against it; the system requires the payment to be reversed first.
